# Red Crab: How to Play

Red Crab is a desktop pet: Clawd and family, three of them, living in the bottom-right corner of your screen. While your AI works, they work along; while you wait for it, they keep you company.

## 1. Getting started

- Once installed, the family appears at the bottom right of the screen.
- At the first start the Connect AI window opens: tick the AIs you use and click Apply.
  - Codex also asks you to trust the hooks in its settings; DeepSeek Harness needs one restart.
- To change it later: right-click one of them → Connect AI….

## 2. What they are telling you

- **The big one works along with the AI**: reading files, editing code, running commands and searching the web each have their own moves.
- **When the AI is done**, it holds up "Done!"; **on an error** it startles or wipes its brow; **when the AI needs your approval** it waves and holds up a sign.
- **The small circles at its feet** are your conversations, one circle each:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Blue | Working |
| Orange | Waiting for you |
| Green | Done |
| Red | Error |
| Grey | You stopped it |

Hover over a circle to see the conversation's name; click it to jump there.

## 3. Playing with them

| You do | They do |
|---|---|
| Click / double-click | Wave / celebrate |
| Drag and let go | Get picked up, then land |
| Drag and fling | Fly off, tumble, and get up dizzy |
| Drop one onto another | High five; on the big one's head, a tower |
| Leave the mouse still beside one | It looks over, comes closer, and dozes off |
| Ctrl + mouse wheel | The whole family grows or shrinks |
| Right-click | The menu |

Most-used menu items: **Rummage, Play with a treasure, Album, Play together** (combos), **Family** (one to three), **Size**, **Activity**.

## 4. Digging and treasures

- **By themselves**: while the AI works, every 2 minutes of work a small one crouches and digs. What it finds is handed to you when the turn ends.
- **By hand**: every 10 minutes of the AI's work earns you one dig; use it with Rummage in the right-click menu. A new family starts with 2.
- There are 28 treasures in four rarities (common, uncommon, rare, special), with a pity guarantee. The second batch of 8 appears at Lv.10. Each rare or special one has a game of its own.
- Take out a treasure you have found with Play with a treasure in the right-click menu.

## 5. Levels and the album

- The family shares one level. XP comes mostly from the AI's work, and also from new discoveries, combos and playing with them. The top is Lv.20: about two weeks to a month of daily use.
- Levels bring gifts: new sign colours and frames, more digs kept, the second batch of treasures, little surprises and more, all the way to Lv.20.
- **The album** (right-click → Album): your collection, your level and the next gift. Things you have not found yet come with a riddle.

## 6. Tips

- Click the little Clawd icon in the taskbar tray to hide or show them. The tray menu can also start them with Windows.
- Too lively? Set Activity to Quiet.
- Light: no internet, no model calls, almost no system resources.
- To uninstall: Windows Settings → Apps → Red Crab. Your save is kept by default, so you can pick up where you left off.
